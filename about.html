LOCAL WORKSPACE
STI COLLEGE ALAMINOS · ACADEMIC RESEARCH

ResearchHub makes academic discovery more organized.

ResearchHub is an academic research workspace shaped by the STI College Alaminos capstone proposal for an AI-powered repository and research retrieval chatbot. It brings project references, personal research tools, and role-based academic workspaces together in one place.

✳
PROJECT OVERVIEW

A practical academic workspace for the STI community

The project responds to the challenge of finding and organizing past academic work. This working application provides searchable project references, a private personal library, and a shared repository where student submissions are reviewed by Teachers or Admins before publication.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

What the study aims to do

  • Analyze challenges in searching for and storing research papers.
  • Determine AI features that can improve document retrieval.
  • Develop a web-based repository with smart search and categorization.
  • Evaluate the proposed system’s performance and usability.
PURPOSE & BENEFITS

Make research easier to access and use

  • Give the STI academic community a clearer starting point for exploring research topics.
  • Reduce time spent organizing and locating relevant references.
  • Help students and faculty keep personal research documents organized.
  • Support more informed academic work through accessible project information.
PLATFORM FEATURES

Tools available in this workspace

  • Browse and search the project’s academic references and topic summaries.
  • Save, preview, and manage personal PDF, DOCX, and TXT documents in a browser library.
  • Submit research for faculty review and discover approved work in the shared repository.
  • Use secure accounts and role-based student, teacher, and administrator dashboards.
  • Manage class assignments and academic records according to account permissions.
  • Ask the website helper how to use ResearchHub; AI availability depends on server configuration.
WHO IT SERVES

Designed for the STI community

  • Students: find papers relevant to studies and projects.
  • Faculty: access past research and manage resources.
  • Administrators: organize institutional files centrally.
  • Future researchers: use the study as a reference for similar systems.
SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

What the proposal covers

The capstone proposal focuses on storing, organizing, and retrieving academic papers, research, and capstone files. It explores AI-assisted search and accounts for Admin, Faculty, and Student roles.

The documents note two limitations: users need an active internet connection, and AI search accuracy depends on the quality and text content of uploaded files.

PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE

Java, C#, and retrieval-augmented generation

The capstone manuscript describes Java (Spring Boot and LangChain4j) for document parsing and background re-indexing, with C# (ASP.NET Core and Semantic Kernel) for API routing and interface controls.

Retrieval-augmented generation and vector embeddings are proposed to support conversational document retrieval. The manuscript also recommends page-level citations and a hybrid dense-vector and sparse BM25 retrieval approach.
